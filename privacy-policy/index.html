Privacy Policy for Good Old Dashcam

Developer: Domnup  ·  Contact: domnup@gmail.com  ·  Effective date: 16 August 2026

Summary: Good Old Dashcam does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app records — video, audio, location and sensor data — stays on your device, in Android app-specific storage. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and the app has no internet permission.

1. What the app does

Good Old Dashcam turns an Android phone into a dashcam. It records loop video while you drive, displays live telemetry (speed, GPS position, G-force), detects driving events such as hard braking or impacts, and keeps a local log of those events. It is designed to work fully offline.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

To provide these features, the app uses the following, all handled locally on your phone:

  • Camera — to record the road ahead as video clips.
  • Microphone (optional) — to record audio alongside video, only if you enable it in Settings.
  • Location (GPS/network) — to show and record speed, heading and coordinates as part of the on-screen telemetry and event log. Location is accessed only for a user-started recording session when GPS telemetry is enabled. It may continue while that foreground recording session is active, including when the screen is locked. The app does not request the Android background-location permission.
  • Motion sensors — the accelerometer and gyroscope, to measure G-force and detect events such as hard braking or a collision.

3. Where your data is stored

All recordings and data are written only to Android app-specific storage on your device (for example, video is saved with getExternalFilesDir and the event log in a local database). This data:

  • is never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to the developer or any third party;
  • is kept in Android app-specific storage; access is controlled by Android unless you explicitly share or export a copy;
  • is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect any of your data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that gather information. The app does not hold the INTERNET permission and cannot upload data off the device on its own.

5. Sharing you initiate yourself

The app lets you export your event log as a CSV file and share it using Android's standard share sheet (for example by email or to cloud storage). This only happens when you choose to do it, and the data goes wherever you send it. Any handling of that file after it leaves the app is governed by the app or service you share it to, not by Good Old Dashcam.

6. Your control

  • You can turn microphone recording on or off in Settings.
  • You can grant or deny camera, microphone and location permissions at any time in your device settings; the app degrades gracefully when a permission is denied.
  • You can delete individual recordings, clear the event log, or use Privacy & Data → Delete local recordings & telemetry. Uninstalling the app removes its app-specific storage. Copies you previously shared or exported must be deleted from their destination separately.

7. Children

Good Old Dashcam is a utility for drivers and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
