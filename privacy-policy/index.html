Privacy Policy for Good Old Dashcam

Developer: Domnup  ·  Contact: domnup@gmail.com  ·  Effective date: 26 September 2026

Summary: Good Old Dashcam does not collect or transmit your recordings or telemetry to the developer. Video, optional audio, optional location and sensor data are processed and stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts, analytics or advertising, and the app has no Internet permission. Files leave the app only when you explicitly choose to share or export them using Android.

1. What the app does

Good Old Dashcam turns an Android phone into a dashcam. It can record loop video and user-requested photos, display live telemetry such as speed, GPS position and G-force, detect events such as hard braking or impacts, protect incident footage, and keep a local event log. It is designed to work fully offline.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

To provide these features, the app may use the following, all handled locally on your phone:

  • Camera — to record dashcam video and capture a JPEG snapshot when you request one.
  • Microphone (optional) — to record audio with video only if you enable audio in Settings and grant microphone permission.
  • Location (GPS/network) — to provide speed, heading, coordinates and GPX route data when GPS telemetry is enabled. Location is accessed only for a user-started recording session and may continue while that foreground recording session remains active, including when the screen is locked. The app does not request Android background-location permission.
  • Motion sensors — accelerometer and gyroscope data used for G-force telemetry, mounting calibration and incident detection.
  • Device status — battery level, charging status, temperature, thermal status and storage availability used locally to display status and protect recording reliability.

3. Where your data is stored

MP4 recordings contain clean camera video without telemetry text burned into the image. Telemetry is stored in separate CSV, GPX and SRT files, including for protected incident clips. Recordings and telemetry are stored in Android app-specific storage on your device. Video is normally written under the app's external-files area, while event information and preferences are kept in local app storage. Depending on enabled features, the app may create MP4 video, GPX route files, telemetry CSV and SRT subtitle files, JPEG snapshots, event records and temporary incident/export packages. You can also save a settings backup as a JSON file to a destination you choose through Android.

  • The app does not upload these files automatically.
  • Good Old Dashcam has no Internet permission.
  • App-specific files are normally removed by Android when the app is uninstalled.
  • Copies that you previously exported or shared are outside Good Old Dashcam's app-specific storage and are not removed automatically when the app is uninstalled or its local data is deleted.

4. Data collection and developer access

The developer does not receive your recordings, audio, location, sensor telemetry or event history through the app. Good Old Dashcam contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system or background upload service. Because the app does not request the INTERNET permission, it cannot independently transmit your dashcam data to the developer or a remote server.

5. Sharing and exporting that you initiate

Good Old Dashcam provides explicit controls that let you move or share copies of locally stored data. Depending on the screen and selected action, this can include:

  • individual MP4 recordings;
  • incident ZIP packages containing a recording and related GPX/CSV/SRT/event information;
  • GPX route, telemetry CSV and SRT subtitle files;
  • event-log CSV exports; and
  • bulk video/telemetry exports and a settings JSON backup to destinations you select through Android's Storage Access Framework.

These actions occur only after you choose to share or export. Android or the destination app/service then handles the transferred copy. Any storage, transmission, retention or further sharing by that destination is governed by its own privacy and security practices, not by Good Old Dashcam.

6. Your controls

  • You can turn microphone recording on or off in Settings.
  • You can turn GPS telemetry on or off in Settings.
  • You can grant or deny camera, microphone and location permissions in Android settings. Camera permission is required for video recording; optional features degrade when their permissions are unavailable.
  • You can lock or unlock recordings, delete individual recordings, clear the event log, and manage loop-storage behaviour.
  • JPEG snapshots are stored in app-specific storage and removed by local-data deletion or uninstall. The current bulk video export does not include snapshots.
  • You can use Privacy & Data → Delete local recordings & telemetry to remove local recordings, telemetry, temporary export packages and saved event history while keeping app settings.
  • You can export copies before uninstalling if you want to keep them. Copies already shared/exported must be deleted separately from their destination.

7. Foreground and screen-locked recording

Recording starts through a visible user action in the app. If you enable the charger-start option, connecting power can also start recording while the main app is open; it does not start camera recording from a closed or background app. While a recording session is active, Android shows a persistent foreground-service notification. Recording, optional audio and optional location telemetry may continue after the app is no longer visible or while the screen is locked, until the session is stopped or Android/device conditions interrupt it.

8. Children

Good Old Dashcam is a utility intended for drivers and is not directed at children. The app does not create user accounts or send personal information to the developer.

9. Security and limitations

App backups are disabled, cleartext network traffic is disabled, app components are private unless Android must launch them, and shared files use scoped content URIs. No mobile application can guarantee that locally stored data is immune from device compromise, operating-system vulnerabilities, physical access or actions taken by other apps after you explicitly share/export a copy.
